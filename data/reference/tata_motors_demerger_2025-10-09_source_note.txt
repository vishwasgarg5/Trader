Verified facts from the company communication:
- The composite scheme became effective on 2025-10-01; the appointed date was 2025-07-01.
- The record date for the commercial-vehicle share entitlement was 2025-10-14.
- Eligible holders were entitled to one TML Commercial Vehicles Limited share for each one Tata Motors share held on the record date.
- The existing Tata Motors share price was to be adjusted through an exchange price-discovery mechanism.
- The commercial-vehicle shares were not available for exchange trading until listing permission was obtained.

Interpretation and limitation:
The 1:1 figure is a share-entitlement ratio, not an OHLCV price-adjustment factor. A continuous historical shareholder-value series must account for both resulting securities and the interval before the commercial-vehicle shares became tradable.
