# Jio Financial Services — temporary Nifty 50 membership event (2023)

This note records the official NSE Indices announcements and an independent NSE Futures & Options circular relevant to the temporary Nifty 50 membership of the Reliance demerged financial-services entity. It is an event-level research note, not a complete 2023 membership reconstruction.

## 17 July 2023: temporary inclusion

- Official source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/2023-07/ind_prs17072023.pdf
- Announcement date: 2023-07-17
- Effective point: 2023-07-20 (close of 2023-07-19)
- NSE Indices describes inclusion of the spun-off entity from Reliance Industries in Nifty 50 and other indices on account of the demerger.
- Before separate listing, the entity is an index placeholder valued using the provider's corporate-action methodology/constant-price treatment. It must not be represented as an ordinary exchange-traded JIOFIN daily price history before listing.
- The official press release PDF is archived and SHA-256 recorded in the special-event register.

## 18 August 2023: placeholder-to-listed identity transition

- Official source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/2023-08/PR_list_18082023.pdf

## 5 September 2023: exclusion of listed JIOFIN

- Official NSE Indices source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/2023-09/ind_prs05092023.pdf
- Announcement date: 2023-09-05
- Effective point: 2023-09-07 (close of 2023-09-06)
- NSE Indices states JIOFIN had not hit the price band on 4 and 5 September and announces exclusion from Nifty 50 and other indices effective 7 September.
- The release adds: if JIOFIN hits the price band on 6 September, exclusion shall not be deferred further. This is a no-later-than clause; it does not leave the published 7 September effective date unresolved.
- Independent exchange corroboration: NSE F&O circular NSE/FAOP/58243, Circular 145/2023, dated 5 September 2023: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP58243.pdf. Its indexed full text confirms JIOFIN exclusion from NIFTY 50 effective 7 September 2023 (close 6 September). The circular PDF itself is located at the official NSE archive URL but is not yet committed or SHA-256 recorded in this repository.
- The primary NSE Indices exclusion PDF is archived and hashed in the special-event register.
